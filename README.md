## Research Repository: Community Driven, Equitable Intervention For Black Children with Autism (Autism Doula Program)

This repository contains all materials related to Study 1 & Study 2 of the Feasibility, Acceptability, and Preliminary Effectiveness of Community Driven, Equitable Intervention For Black Children with Autism conducted by Josselyn Nia Aldridge and Teresa Smith at Cincinnati Children’s Hospital Medical Center. The study evaluates a first-of-its-kind autism doula program.

This repository contains the data, R analysis scripts, required to replicate the analysis and findings for the manuscript/study report.
